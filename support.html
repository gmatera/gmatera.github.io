Support

Questions and problems with any Steadyseam app reach the developer who built it, in English or Italian.

Contact

E-mail: support@steadyseam.com

Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 Central European Time (Europe/Rome), except Italian public holidays.

Response: first answer within one business day. Problems that stop an app from working for everyone come first.

What to include

  • the name of the app and the address of your site (for example your-company.atlassian.net);
  • what you expected and what happened instead, with screenshots if you can.

Never send passwords, secret tokens or API keys. We will never ask for them.

On-Prem Git & SVN for Jira Cloud

Please also include:

  • a screenshot of the app page in Jira (Settings > Apps > On-Prem Git & SVN), including Needs attention if it shows anything;
  • for GitLab: the response shown in the system hook's Recent events;
  • for Subversion: the output of the status command of the connector hook.

Ops Essentials for Jira Service Management

Please also include:

  • a screenshot of the Overview tab of the app (Settings > Apps > Ops Essentials);
  • for heartbeats: the name of the heartbeat and the time of its last call;
  • for stakeholder updates: the result shown in Recent updates.

Never send webhook addresses or API keys.

Security issues

Report vulnerabilities privately to support@steadyseam.com and please do not disclose them publicly before we have answered. We acknowledge reports within two business days and keep you informed until the issue is fixed. Each app's security page describes how the app is built.

Your data

We use what you send us only to answer you, as described in our privacy information.
